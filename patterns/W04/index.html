01

Pointcut

Where the mechanism applies. All four dimensions belong to the proposed match, not just its vocabulary.

Context
A lead agent can generate tasks rather than call a fixed pipeline.
Trigger
The orchestrator decomposes a goal or dispatches a subtask.
Structure
Task plan, worker selection, delegation envelope, and synthesis step.
Effect
Synthesized output accounts for generated task results and delegated authority.
02

Protected contract

The obligation that clients, operators, and auditors should be able to rely on.

Requires
Workers advertise capabilities and share a task/result protocol.
Ensures
Every dispatched task has a result or failure and the final answer links them.
Invariant
Dynamic dispatch cannot create unbounded uncounted worker expansion.
Violation
Unsplittable, failed, or duplicate work is reflected in synthesis.

Negative control

A fixed sequence of sub-agents is W01 rather than dynamic W04.

03

Variation & lifecycle

The hot spot is admitted only where the protected semantics survive.

Permitted variation

Planner-executor, manager-worker, sub-agent registry, and dynamic graph.

Test obligation

Test decomposition quality, worker failure, duplicate task, and recursion cap.

Evolution question

Did fixed chains evolve into runtime task planning?
