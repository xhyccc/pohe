01

Pointcut

Where the mechanism applies. All four dimensions belong to the proposed match, not just its vocabulary.

Context
Producers and consumers are decoupled through topics.
Trigger
A message is published to a topic.
Structure
Topic schema, broker or bus, subscribers, and delivery policy.
Effect
Matching subscribers receive the message under declared ordering and durability rules.
02

Protected contract

The obligation that clients, operators, and auditors should be able to rely on.

Requires
Topic identity and event compatibility are defined.
Ensures
Delivery or drop is observable and subscriber selection is attributable.
Invariant
A subscriber cannot read a topic outside its authorization.
Violation
Duplicate, late, or lost delivery follows an explicit semantic contract.

Negative control

Directly invoking known listeners is C02 rather than C03.

03

Variation & lifecycle

The hot spot is admitted only where the protected semantics survive.

Permitted variation

In-process bus, OS socket pubsub, cloud queue, and durable stream.

Test obligation

Test multi-subscriber delivery, no subscriber, disconnect, duplicate, and auth.

Evolution question

Did pubsub gain durability, replay, and authorization?
