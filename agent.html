THE PIPELINE

Three moves, three questions.

What is worth reading? Is the mechanism in this scope? What may safely change?

01 / DETECT

Where are the leads?

Run the frozen cue expressions over code, tests, and support files. Excludes caches, build output, and vendored trees.

pohe-agent scan <repo>
02 / ANALYZE

Is the mechanism here?

Anchor each cue to its enclosing declaration, then check context, trigger, structure, and effect. The packet reports missing roles, the negative control, and contract probes.

pohe-agent analyze <repo> --pattern P01
03 / EVOLVE

What may change?

Build a proposal that protects the contract, names authority, adds the test obligation, and records rollback.

pohe-agent plan <repo> --findings findings.json

EVIDENCE LADDER

Cues are leads.
Contracts are claims.

The tool can produce E1 on its own. E2 and E3 require reading the source, the tests, or a runtime check. The package keeps that boundary visible in every report.

    Proposed recordThe frozen specification and its negative control.
  2. E1
    Retrieval cueA regex or symbol hit. Produced by scan and match.
  3. E2

  4. E3

AGENT WORKFLOW

From scan to recorded decision.

  1. 01

    Orient

    Read the target repository's own instructions and confirm the task: detection only, matching, or evolution.

  2. 02

    Scan

    Retrieve cues, read the family table, and keep unobserved records visible as unobserved.

  3. 03

    Analyze

    Run analyze, read the anchored scope and missing roles, then open the source. Answer all four pointcut dimensions and all four contract fields. Apply the negative control.

  4. 04

    Record

    Write the evidence level actually reached, the files and tests, and the remaining unknowns.

  5. 05

    Plan

    Protect the contract, stay inside the variation seam, and name authority, rollback, and a retirement trigger.

  6. 06

    Verify

    Add the contract test first. Exercise failure and termination paths. Canary one real task before rollout.
